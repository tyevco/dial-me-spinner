9. Drop shock on the 608 and flick reactions
==============================================================================
608 pure-axial static limit = 0.25*C0 = 342 N [verifier].  Rotor 136 g:
drop m  F @ 0.5 mm stop  F @ 1 mm  F @ 2 mm
------  ---------------  --------  --------
0.1     267              133       67      
0.3     800              400       200     
0.75    1999             1000      500     
-> rigid seating cannot protect the 608 beyond ~0.1 m.  Design: outer race held by a wave spring
   (30-50 N) under a printed retainer; above that the carrier lands on the POM spacer flange 0.15 mm
   below (crash stop), so balls see <= spring force.  Brinelling still possible on edge impacts.
Flick to 10 rev/s: lateral impulse 0.218 N*s -> ~7.3 N average over 30 ms vs slide threshold mu*W = 1.3-2.2 N: the puck slides unless held or on micro-suction pads.
